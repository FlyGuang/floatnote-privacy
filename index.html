感谢你使用 FloatNote（以下简称“本应用”）。本隐私政策说明我们如何处理与你的隐私相关的信息。我们致力于以透明、简单的方式说明这一点。

1. 我们收集的信息

我们不收集、不访问、不传输任何个人信息。

  • 本应用是一款本地浮动便签应用，你的笔记内容仅存储在你自己设备的本地。
  • 我们不会收集你的姓名、邮箱、位置、设备标识等任何可识别个人身份的信息。
  • 本应用不包含账户系统，无需注册或登录。

2. 本地数据处理

你在本应用中创建的所有笔记、设置和偏好，均只保存在你的设备本地存储中。我们无法访问这些内容，也不会将它们上传到任何服务器。卸载应用后，本地数据可能随之删除，请自行做好备份。

3. 第三方服务

本应用不集成任何第三方广告、分析、统计或数据追踪服务，也不会向任何第三方提供数据。

4. 系统权限

本应用仅使用运行所需的最基本系统能力（如本地文件读写），不会申请与核心功能无关的权限（如通讯录、摄像头、麦克风、位置等）。

5. 数据安全

由于本应用不收集、不上传任何数据，因此不存在服务器端数据泄露的风险。你的数据安全由你所在设备的本地安全机制保障。

6. 未成年人隐私

本应用不面向未成年人收集任何信息，亦不包含任何要求提供个人信息的场景，适用于所有年龄用户正常使用。

7. 你的权利

由于我们不持有你的任何数据，因此无需提供数据查询、修改或删除申请渠道。你可以随时通过卸载应用来删除本应用产生的全部本地数据。

8. 政策变更

如果我们未来对本隐私政策进行更改，将更新本页面并修改上述“生效日期”。若未来应用新增任何数据收集功能，我们会在显著位置告知并重新发布本政策。

Thank you for using FloatNote (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information related to your privacy. We are committed to doing so in a transparent and simple way.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, access, or transmit any personal information.

  • The App is a local floating notes application. Your notes are stored only locally on your own device.
  • We do not collect any personally identifiable information, such as your name, email address, location, or device identifiers.
  • The App has no account system and requires no registration or login.

2. Local Data Processing

All notes, settings, and preferences you create in the App are stored only in the local storage of your device. We cannot access this content and will never upload it to any server. After you uninstall the App, local data may be deleted; please back up your data as needed.

3. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party advertising, analytics, statistics, or tracking services, and does not provide any data to third parties.

4. System Permissions

The App only uses the most basic system capabilities required for its operation (such as local file reading and writing) and does not request permissions unrelated to its core functionality (such as contacts, camera, microphone, location, etc.).

5. Data Security

Because the App does not collect or upload any data, there is no risk of server-side data breaches. Your data security is safeguarded by the local security mechanisms of your device.

6. Children’s Privacy

The App does not collect any information from minors and contains no scenarios that require personal information. It is suitable for users of all ages.

7. Your Rights

Since we do not hold any of your data, there is no need to provide channels for data access, correction, or deletion requests. You can delete all local data generated by the App at any time by uninstalling it.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this Privacy Policy in the future, we will update this page and revise the “Effective Date” above. If the App adds any new data collection features in the future, we will notify you in a prominent location and republish this policy.
